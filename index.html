Our Story

Made With Love, By a Home Cook Who Missed Home Too

GreenBite Meals began in 2021 in a small Abu Dhabi kitchen, when founder Lalit Singh — a homesick expat working long hours — started cooking a little extra dal and sabzi each evening for a few colleagues who missed proper home food. Word spread fast.

What started as tiffins for five friends is now a trusted daily meal service for hundreds of professionals and families across the city. Every recipe is still Lalit's own — passed down from his grandmother's kitchen in Jaipur — made fresh each morning with the same care as day one.

Our mission has never changed: give people who are far from home a plate of food that actually tastes like it.

Lalit Singh Founder & Head Chef, GreenBite Meals
